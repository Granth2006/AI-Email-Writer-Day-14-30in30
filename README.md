## ⚙️ Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>⚡ Blazing Fast Generation</h3>
      Powered by Groq's Llama-3.3-70b-versatile model for nearly instant email drafting.
    </td>
    <td width="50%">
      <h3>🎛️ Highly Customizable</h3>
      Fine-tune your email's tone, length, type, and target audience for the perfect message.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <h3>🕒 Local History Tracking</h3>
      Automatically saves your recently generated emails to localStorage for easy retrieval.
    </td>
    <td>

      Easily copy the generated subject line and body text directly to your clipboard.

## 🔐 Privacy First

> **Your API key and data stay on your device.**
> All generations use your personal Groq API key, which is securely saved in your browser's local storage. None of your data, prompts, or generated emails are sent to any centralized database—it's strictly between your browser and the Groq API.
